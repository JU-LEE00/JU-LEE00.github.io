Introduction

About me

Master’s-level semiconductor engineer specializing in Cu-Cu hybrid bonding, advanced 3D packaging, and surface/interface defect analysis. Experienced in thermo-mechanical FEM, CPFEM, and phase-field modeling, with the ability to correlate simulation outputs with SAT, TEM, AFM, and EBSD-based characterization data. Demonstrated ability to analyze process–microstructure–defect relationships, including void closure, grain-boundary behavior, crystallographic orientation effects, and interface defect evolution. Targeting product, process, or application engineering roles in semiconductor equipment or advanced packaging, where simulation-to-experiment expertise supports process optimization and defect analysis.
RESEARCH

Research

  1. 01

    Cu-Cu Hybrid Bonding Interface Modeling and Defect Analysis

  2. 02

    Investigated Cu-Cu bonding interface evolution and void closure mechanisms for 3D semiconductor integration using FEM/CPFEM simulations and experimental correlation.

  3. 03

    Evaluated the effects of temperature, pressure, bonding conditions, and surface morphology on interface deformation, local void behavior, and bonding quality.

  4. 04

    Applied crystal plasticity-based modeling to analyze microstructure-dependent deformation behavior at the Cu-Cu bonding interface.

  5. 05

    Analyzed crystallographic orientation, grain-boundary structures, and grain-boundary junction effects on bonding interface formation and defect evolution.

  6. 06

    Correlated simulation results with SAT, TEM, AFM, and EBSD observations to support mechanism-based interpretation of surface/interface defect behavior.

  7. 07

    Hybrid Bonding Process and Microstructural Defect Analysis

  8. 08

    Analyzed hybrid bonding process conditions and their influence on microstructural defect formation in semiconductor packaging structures.

  9. 09

    Used AFM/SPM data to evaluate surface morphology and relate surface characteristics to bonding interface quality.

  10. 10

    Interpreted EBSD-based crystallographic information to analyze grain orientation effects, grain-boundary behavior, and microstructure-dependent bonding responses.

  11. 11

    Integrated experimental characterization and computational modeling to support quantitative interpretation of bonding interface behavior.

  12. 12

    Prepared technical reports, research presentations, and conference materials summarizing simulation results, experimental observations, and process-related defect mechanisms.
